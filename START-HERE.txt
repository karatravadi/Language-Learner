STILUS: SETUP (Windows, about 5 minutes, everything free)
=========================================================

You need ONE thing installed: Node.js. It is free and runs the little local
server behind the app. Nothing else.

STEP 1  Install Node.js (once)
   - Go to https://nodejs.org and download the "LTS" version.
   - Run the installer and click Next through it (the defaults are fine).
   - Check it worked: press the Windows key, type "cmd", press Enter, then type
         node --version
     You should see v18 or higher (e.g. v22.x.x).

STEP 2  Get the app
   - On https://github.com/karatravadi/Language-Learner click Code > Download ZIP,
     right-click the zip, choose "Extract All", and put the folder somewhere
     permanent (e.g. Documents). Do NOT run it from inside the zip.
     (Or, with Git: git clone https://github.com/karatravadi/Language-Learner.git)

STEP 3  Start it
   - Open the folder and double-click  start.bat
   - A black window opens and your browser opens the app at
     http://localhost:3000
   - Keep the black window open while you study. Close it to stop the app.
   - If Windows SmartScreen warns about start.bat: click "More info", then
     "Run anyway". (It only runs "node server.js".)

That's it. Every chapter of the Spanish, French and Latin courses, the word of
the day, quizzes and spaced-repetition review work right now, with NO account,
key or internet needed. Switch course at the top of the home screen.

Your day: the home screen plans about 45 minutes (word of the day, review, a
chapter, then weak spots or AI chat). Change the goal in Settings (gear icon).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTIONAL: switch on the AI tutor (corrects your own sentences, chats in character)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 4  Get a free API key
   Option A (recommended, free tier): Google Gemini
     - Go to https://aistudio.google.com and sign in with a Google account.
     - Click "Get API key" and create one. Copy it.
     - Free-tier limits are daily and Google does not publish them; developers
       report roughly 20-500 requests a day depending on model. Google says
       free-tier prompts may be used to improve its products, so don't type
       anything private into the AI parts.
   Option B: DeepSeek
     - https://platform.deepseek.com , create an account and an API key.
     - New accounts have reportedly had a small free credit, then it is
       pay-as-you-go. Check their pricing page for the current price.

STEP 5  Put the key in settings.env
   - Copy settings.example.env and name the copy settings.env (once).
   - Right-click settings.env, "Open with" > Notepad.
   - For Gemini: paste your key after  GEMINI_API_KEY=   and keep PROVIDER=gemini
     For DeepSeek: paste after  DEEPSEEK_API_KEY=   and set PROVIDER=deepseek
   - No quotes, no spaces. Save the file.

STEP 6  Restart
   - Close the black window, then double-click start.bat again.
   - At the bottom of the home screen it should now say "AI tutor on".

If the AI says it can't find the model: open Google AI Studio, copy the exact
name of a "Flash-Lite" model, and paste it after GEMINI_MODEL= in settings.env.
(The default name in the file is my best guess; I could not confirm it.)

---------------------------------------------------------------------
GOOD TO KNOW
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Your progress (XP, streak, review cards) is saved in the browser you use, at
  the address http://localhost:3000 . Use the same browser and the same port
  each time. A different browser or a changed PORT looks like a fresh start.
- Audio: the speaker buttons use your computer's built-in Spanish voice.
  Microsoft Edge usually has the best Spanish voices. If you see no speaker
  buttons, Windows has no Spanish voice installed: Settings > Time & language >
  Speech > Add voices. (Listening exercises are skipped until a voice exists.)
- "Port 3000 is already in use": the trainer is probably already open in
  another black window. Close it, or change PORT in settings.env.
- "node is not recognized": restart your computer after installing Node.js.
- Your API key never leaves your computer except to go to Google/DeepSeek.
